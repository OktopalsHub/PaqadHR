# Cookie Notice

**Last modified:** 20 October 2026

This Cookie Notice explains how Oktopals Limited ("**Oktopals Limited**," "**we**," "**us**," or "**our**"), operating Paqad, uses cookies and similar technologies on paqadhr.com and other sites or apps that link to this notice (the "**Sites**").

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the Sites function, keep you signed in, remember preferences, and (where used) understand how the product is used.

We may use session cookies (deleted when you close the browser) and persistent cookies (stored until they expire or you delete them). Cookies may be first-party (set by Oktopals Limited / Paqad) or third-party (set by providers that help us operate the Sites).

## Cookies and similar technologies we use

| Type | Purpose | Examples | How to control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential | Required for sign-in, security, load balancing, and core product operation | Auth session cookies (httpOnly), CSRF/security tokens | Required for the Service; block via browser may prevent sign-in |
| Attribution | Remember which campaign or website brought a visitor to Paqad so we can measure marketing | `paqad_attribution` (first-party, 90 days, stores UTM parameters and ad click identifiers) | Browser cookie controls; contact info@paqadhr.com |
| Analytics | Understand product usage and improve Paqad | PostHog | Browser cookie controls; contact info@paqadhr.com |
| Functionality / diagnostics | Detect errors and improve reliability | Sentry | Browser controls; contact info@paqadhr.com |

We do **not** set advertising or social-media advertising cookies, and we do not use advertising cookies to target ads. When you follow a campaign link, we do record the campaign parameters and ad click identifiers from that link (for example `utm_source`, `gclid`, or `fbclid`) in our first-party attribution cookie and analytics so we can measure which campaigns bring visitors to Paqad.

## Other technologies

- **Browser storage.** We may use local or session storage for similar purposes as cookies (for example, UI preferences).
- **Pixels / beacons.** We may use pixels in transactional or product emails to understand delivery and engagement.
- **SDKs.** If a mobile app is offered, third-party SDKs may collect device and usage data for analytics or crash reporting under this notice.

We do not use session-replay tools that record keystrokes.

## Your choices

1. **Browser controls.** Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking essential cookies may stop sign-in or other core features.
2. **Analytics.** You can limit analytics cookies through your browser. Email info@paqadhr.com if you need help with an analytics opt-out request.
3. **Do not track / GPC.** We do not use advertising cookies. If you send a Global Privacy Control signal, we will treat it as a request not to sell or share personal information for targeted advertising where that concept applies.

For more on how we process personal information, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
