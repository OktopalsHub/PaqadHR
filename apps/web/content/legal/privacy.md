# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 16 October 2026**

Oktopals Limited ("Paqad", "PaqadHR", "we", "us", or "our") provides PaqadHR, a people-operations service for organisations. This policy explains how personal data is handled when you visit our website, create an account, contact us, or use PaqadHR.

This policy is a general description of our service. The features enabled by an organisation and the way that organisation uses PaqadHR affect which data is processed.

## 1. When we are the controller and when we are the processor

We act as a **data controller** for information we use to operate our own business, such as account registration, customer contacts, billing administration, support requests, website security, and marketing communications we send.

When an organisation uses PaqadHR to manage its employees, applicants, payroll, attendance, leave, or recognition, that organisation generally decides why and how those records are used. In those cases, the organisation is the **controller** and Oktopals Limited generally processes the records on its instructions. Employees and applicants should contact their organisation first about those records. Our [Data Processing Addendum](/dpa) describes the processor relationship.

## 2. Personal data we may process

Depending on how you interact with PaqadHR and which features your organisation enables, the data may include:

- **Account and contact information:** name, work email, organisation, role, login details, and account preferences.
- **Workforce information:** employee profiles, job titles, departments, employment records, leave requests, attendance, schedules, and documents entered by an organisation.
- **Recruitment information:** job applications, CVs, candidate contact details, interview notes, and hiring decisions entered by an organisation.
- **Payroll and payment information:** salary and payroll records, tax or statutory identifiers, payout instructions, bank or payout destination details, transaction references, and payroll-run history. Payment providers may handle payment details directly.
- **Communications:** support messages, feedback, contact-form submissions, and other information you choose to send us.
- **Technical and usage information:** device and browser details, IP address, logs, diagnostic events, and service-usage information used for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.
- **Integration information:** data made available when an organisation connects an integration or uses a third-party sign-in option, subject to that provider's settings and terms.

Please do not submit information that is not needed for the relevant PaqadHR feature.

## 3. How we use personal data

We may use personal data to:

- provide, maintain, and secure PaqadHR and its features;
- create and administer accounts and organisations;
- support recruitment, workforce management, leave, attendance, payroll, and recognition features requested by an organisation;
- process subscriptions, payments, or payouts when those features are enabled;
- respond to support requests and communicate service notices;
- diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and investigate security incidents;
- understand service reliability and improve product usability;
- meet legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not use an organisation's workforce data for our own unrelated purposes. Where we act as processor, we handle that data according to the organisation's documented instructions and applicable law.

## 4. Legal grounds for processing

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on the basis that applies to the relevant purpose. This may include performing a contract, complying with a legal obligation, pursuing legitimate interests such as service security and support, or consent where required. Organisations using PaqadHR are responsible for identifying and communicating the legal basis for workforce and candidate data they control.

## 5. When we share personal data

We may share relevant data with:

- **The organisation you belong to:** administrators and authorised users may access information according to their organisation's configuration and permissions.
- **Service providers:** providers supporting hosting, storage, authentication, communications, diagnostics, security, and service operation, where used.
- **Payment and payout providers:** providers such as Bachs, Nomba, Monnify, Fincra, or Noah may process relevant payment or payout data when the organisation has enabled a feature that uses them. The provider and data involved depend on the configuration and transaction.
- **Integrations you enable:** third-party services selected by an organisation or user.
- **Authorities or other parties where required:** where disclosure is required by law, necessary to protect rights or safety, or connected to a legal claim or business transfer.

We do not sell personal data for money. We do not use workforce or payroll data to serve third-party advertising. If our practices change, we will update this policy as required by law.

## 6. International transfers

PaqadHR may use service providers or infrastructure in countries other than the country where you or your organisation are located. Where a cross-border transfer is subject to data protection requirements, we take steps intended to use an applicable transfer mechanism and safeguards. Contact us if you need information about a transfer relevant to your use of the service.

## 7. Retention and deletion

We keep personal data for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, to provide the service, and to meet applicable legal, accounting, security, or dispute-resolution requirements.

For workforce and payroll records that we process on an organisation's behalf, the organisation is generally responsible for deciding retention periods and requesting deletion, subject to applicable law and technical or contractual requirements. Some records may remain in backups or be retained where the law requires. To request deletion of your Paqad account or ask a privacy question, email **info@paqadhr.com**. If the request concerns records controlled by your employer or organisation, we may direct you to that organisation.

## 8. Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Organisations are responsible for assigning appropriate access permissions and protecting their users' account credentials.

If you believe there is a security or privacy issue involving PaqadHR, contact **info@paqadhr.com**.

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

PaqadHR may use cookies or similar technologies needed for authentication, security, preferences, and service operation. Where analytics or other optional technologies are enabled, their use is subject to applicable requirements and any choices presented to you. See our [Cookie Notice](/cookies) for more information.

## 10. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or a copy of your personal data; to object to certain processing; or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To exercise a right regarding data for which Oktopals Limited is the controller, email **info@paqadhr.com**. We may need to verify your identity before responding. If your request concerns employee, applicant, or payroll data controlled by your organisation, please contact that organisation first; we will assist it as required by our processor obligations.

If you are in Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable Nigeria Data Protection Commission requirements may apply. If you are in the EEA or United Kingdom, applicable GDPR or UK GDPR rights may apply. You may also have rights under other local laws. You can complain to the relevant data protection authority if you believe your rights have been infringed.

## 11. Children

PaqadHR is designed for organisational and workplace use, not for children to create personal accounts independently. Organisations must ensure that any personal data they submit is collected and used lawfully.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our service, providers, or legal obligations change. We will update the effective date when we publish a revised version. If a change requires notice under applicable law, we will provide that notice through an appropriate channel.
